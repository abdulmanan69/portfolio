How I work

The same three steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Work out what's actually needed

    We talk about the problem, not the feature list. You get back a plan: what I'd build first, what I'd leave out, and how long the first working version takes.

    A day or two
  2. 02

    Build it where you can watch

    It goes on a real URL early and gets updated as I work. You try it while changes are still cheap, instead of seeing everything at the end.

    Most of the project
  3. 03

    Ship it and hand it over

    Deployed on your domain, with the repository, the deploy steps and a walkthrough. Everything runs on your accounts — nothing is locked to me.

    Yours to keep
